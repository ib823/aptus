THE PRODUCT  (one paragraph)
---------------------------
The workbench is a Fit-to-Standard pre-onboarding tool. Before the Explore-phase
Fit-to-Standard workshops, an executive sponsor affirms the SAP scoping decisions
in plain language, so the workshops confirm rather than discover. The consultant
assembles a scope bundle, the client affirms it, the consultant reviews and
releases, and the workbench produces a signed affirmation record and a pre-sorted
workshop agenda. It does not configure SAP — it produces affirmation outputs.

DATA MODEL
----------
  ValueStream    id, name, isFoundation
  SubProcess     id, name, streamId, type (functional | industry | catch-all)
  ScopeItem      id, description, subProcessId, sapBusinessArea,
                 hasBdcCoverage, placementReviewFlag (nullable)
  AffirmQuestion id (L2-0xx), scopeItemRefs[], streamId, subProcessId,
                 sapVerbatim, plainLanguageSuggested, consultantWording,
                 sapArea, sapTopic, sscuiRef, sourceQuestionnaire,
                 status (suggested | confirmed | excluded), flag (nullable)
  Bundle         id, client, scopeItemIds[], affirmQuestionIds[],
                 state (draft | issued | submitted | released)
  Response       affirmQuestionId, choice (standard | discuss | deviate),
                 reason (required when deviate)
Import the four spreadsheets into this model. Layer 1 "Full Hierarchy" is the
source of truth for ScopeItem -> SubProcess -> Stream. Layer 2 + the plain-language
file are the source of truth for AffirmQuestion.

THE FOUR SCREENS  (build to the fetched design — input 5)
--------------------------------------------------------
Screen 1 — Consultant · scope. A value-stream tree picker over the 672-item
  master: 8 streams + Foundation, expandable to sub-processes and scope items.
  Manual selection (no DDA import). Assembles the affirm-set from the selected
  scope. Coverage shown honestly — sub-processes with no BDC questions are still
  selectable but marked as carrying no affirm-set.
Screen 2 — Client · affirm. The workbench header + live scorecard, then the
  affirm cards grouped by sub-process and SAP Area. Each card shows the
  plain-language prompt prominently, the SAP verbatim beneath in a labelled
  "source of truth" box, three choice chips, and a reason field on deviation.
  Defaults to "Adopt SAP standard". Client submits; answers seal.
Screen 3 — Consultant · review & release. THE GATE. Affirmation summary, sealed
  status, the deviations and discuss items laid out for review, the flagged-
  question note, and the release control. Nothing is produced or sent until the
  consultant releases. Build this screen in the most depth.
Screen 4 — Consultant · output. The signed affirmation record and the pre-sorted
  Fit-to-Standard workshop agenda (fast-confirm / discuss / deviations-needing-
  design). Produced only after release; the consultant sends them manually.

BUILD LOGIC — the six locked decisions, as requirements
-------------------------------------------------------
1. SAP's BDC L1/L2/L3 model. The workbench captures L2 only — the pre-workshop
   affirm-set. Do not build L1 (Discovery) or L3 (in-workshop).
2. Manual scope intake against the validated 672-item master. A controlled
   picker, not free text and not an external import.
3. Dual display, always. SAP verbatim is shown on every question and is never
   overwritten or hidden. The generator drafts the plain-language wording; the
   consultant confirms it (status suggested -> confirmed) before client-facing use.
4. Default-to-standard. Every affirm item defaults to "Adopt SAP standard". A
   deviation must be explicitly chosen and must capture a reason.
5. Coverage gap shown, not hidden. 152 of 672 scope items carry BDC questions.
   Make clear which selected scope has an affirm-set and which does not.
6. Two pre-production gates: (a) consultant review-and-release before any client
   sees output; (b) all client delivery is manual. No auto-send, ever.

CAVEATS CCC MUST RESPECT
------------------------
- The 150 L2 questions are not 150 usable questions. 15 are recommended for
  EXCLUSION (14 mistagged config rows in the Quality Management questionnaire
  with no question text; 1 Finance row whose text is literally "Not Applicable").
  Honour the Status field — never show an excluded row to a client. The genuine
  affirm-set is ~135.
- A further 13 questions are flagged as configuration how-to rather than business
  decisions. They are drafted but carry a flag — surface the flag to the consultant.
- The 63 Layer 0 curation-review placements and the plain-language wording are not
  yet consultant-signed-off. Treat the data as release-candidate; render a
  "pending review" state where appropriate.
- The BDC questionnaires are ~2024 vintage and have not been re-verified against
  live SAP 2602. This re-verification is a mandatory gate before client-facing
  launch — do not present the affirm-set as final until it is cleared.

WHAT CCC MUST NOT DO
--------------------
- Do not fabricate SAP content, scope items, questions, or SSCUI references.
- Do not invent UI. Reuse the PR1-PR5 design system and the screens in the UI
  file. Every colour must be a PR1 token.
- Do not auto-configure or write to any SAP tenant. Central Business
  Configuration governs Public Cloud configuration.
- Do not auto-send anything to a client.
- Do not collapse the SAP verbatim into the plain-language version.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
-------------------
- All 672 scope items navigable as the 3-tier tree; counts reconcile.
- The affirm flow renders every non-excluded L2 question with dual display and
  default-to-standard; deviations require a reason.
- Screen 3 blocks all output and delivery behind an explicit consultant release.
- Screen 4 outputs (signed record + agenda) generate only post-release.
- Every screen matches the fetched design (input 5) and uses PR1 tokens only.
- Excluded and flagged questions behave per the caveats above.

PENDING — consultant actions, not CCC's
---------------------------------------
- Sign-off of the 63 Layer 0 curation-review placements.
- Consultant confirm of the 150 plain-language rows (status -> confirmed).
- BDC re-verification against live SAP 2602 (needs SAP partner login).

Build the data layer, the four screens, and the journey logic against this spec.
Where the data carries a flag or a pending state, render it honestly rather than
hiding it — that visibility is the purpose of the tool.
