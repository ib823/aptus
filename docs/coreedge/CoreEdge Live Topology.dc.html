State vocabulary
✓ Observed-goodA success was recorded.
✕ Observed-badA failure was recorded — timeout, refusal, 4xx/5xx. Double stroke.
∅ Never-observedNo call or probe recorded. Not “healthy”. Always still.
— UnobservableCannot be known, permanently. Em-dash, never a zero.
▲ DefectExactly four things, never more: a settled grant with no expiry (“a defect, not a state”), a solution with an empty owner slot, an interface with no entity set, a connection with no declared environment. A standing condition, not an alert — and never correct behaviour.
Ended — a modifier, not a state
✓ Revoked, expired, rejected or superseded. The node keeps the colour and glyph of what was observed — struck through, dashed and inert. A revoked grant that carried traffic stays green; one that never did stays grey. Revocation is not a re-decision — the decision stands in the ledger and the withdrawal is recorded beside it.
Motion

One travelling pulse per recorded call in the window, replayed once. Nothing loops. Nothing idles. The platform cannot assert that anything is alive, so nothing on this canvas pretends to be.

Reduced motion: pulses are replaced by a static “≥ n” count on the edge.

What this view deliberately does not show

  • No summary tiles, health score or “n% healthy”. A second copy of a number is a thing that disagrees with it — Broker traffic /operations/traffic is authoritative for volume.
  • No latency, error rate or uptime. This canvas carries structure, relationships and provenance only.
  • No 90-day window. The server clamps at 30 days; offering 90 would be a control that silently does nothing.
  • No sixth state for endings. Revoked, expired and rejected are a modifier on what was observed — a revoked grant that carried traffic must not be filed beside one that never did.
  • No count of throttled calls. IP-keyed buckets are not attributable to a tenant — em-dash, not zero.
  • No inferred environment for an UNDECLARED connection. A guess rendered in the same type as a fact becomes a fact.
  • No “last seen” on never-observed nodes. The field is blank because the record does not exist.
  • No arrows implying live flow. Direction here is authorisation and call direction, not a heartbeat.
