CoreEdge Console · design addendum to dc2

Four things the Operations Center needs that dc2 does not contain

Everything here is dc2's component language and the production tokens, verbatim. Nothing is redesigned. Each specimen names the payload field it renders, because the endpoints already decide what is true — the design's only job is to not overstate it.

D1

The provenance treatment

Six screens carry a permanent statement that a number is incomplete and why. This is the highest-leverage decision in the set: it is where honest status becomes design language or degrades into fine print.

It is furniture, not an alarm. These notes are permanent properties of the data, seen on every visit. Rendered in amber they would read as unresolved warnings and be mentally dismissed within a week. So the strip uses --surface-ink-tint — the same fill as a table header. It belongs to the component rather than interrupting it.

The claim is the headline. Each strip opens with its assertion in caption type, not the word "note" or an icon. Floor, not census is scannable, specific, and cannot be mistaken for decoration. The explanation follows at 12px.

It sits inside the card, below the content, above nothing. A page-level banner gets scrolled past; a strip attached to the thing it qualifies cannot be read separately from it.

A number with a stated basis never appears alone. latency.medianMs carries basis and sampleSize; the line beneath the figure is not optional and does not disappear when the sample is complete — it reads over 4,182 of 4,182. The em-dash holds the slot at full number size. A greyed zero would read as "throttled"; a spinner would read as "loading"; an empty box would read as broken. An em-dash at 26px says the slot is real and the value is not, permanently. observable: false is the only input.

Two of the four throttle buckets are keyed by an IP address we cannot enumerate and fire before the route runs. Their limit is knowable; their usage is not, ever.

This says nothing crossed a threshold in what the audit feed recorded — not that everything is well. Calls throttled at the edge, calls that timed out before an audit write, and calls whose audit write failed leave no row, and the feed thins exactly when the database is struggling.

The empty state does not say "All clear" or show a green tick. That is the single most consequential piece of copy in the Operations Center: it is the screen most likely to be glanced at, and the glance most likely to be wrong. "Nothing crossed a threshold" is what the data supports.

D2

Binding unverified, and the four-way agreement

A connection whose SAP environment was never declared is neither healthy-and-known nor broken. Reads through it are served but cannot be attributed to a landscape; writes are refused outright.

Absence gets a dashed outline, and nothing else does. The product's existing rule is that an unknown environment renders no chip at all, never a guessed one — correct, and it leaves a blank cell that reads as a rendering fault. A dashed pill with no fill is visibly a deliberate gap: it occupies the chip's position, states what is missing, and cannot be confused with a filled status. No new colour is introduced, which is the point — an absence is not a state.

The same device carries NEVER_TESTED in D4. One idea, two uses.

A progress bar, not a badge — and the target is printed. The brief's requirement is that this reads as work trending to zero rather than a number that could sit constant forever without anyone noticing. A bar with 9 of 12 beside it makes a stalled backlog visible the way a bare count does not. The remediation link is part of the component, not a separate action: a backlog you cannot act on from where you see it does not get cleared.

Specimen · environment agreement, four outcomes
Environment binding · 4,182 calls
agreed · 3,910 unverified · 4 mismatch · 2 no connection reached · 266

Mismatch means a call was served from a landscape other than the one its credential declared. No connection reached means the call was refused at authentication, the throttle, or the grant gate — there was no binding to agree or disagree.

Four, not three — and the fourth is the one that was silently wrong. notApplicable exists because refused calls used to be counted as agreed, so a window of nothing but 401s and 429s reported a wall of agreement. It renders in the neutral grey used for expired, not in a failure colour: those calls were correctly refused, and colouring them red would invent an incident.

Every chip carries its label. Status is never conveyed by colour alone — the three states most at risk of colour-only encoding are exactly these.

D3

Thin, not empty

These screens will hold one credential and two connections before they hold a hundred. One row must look intentional, not like a table that failed to load.

Specimen · a table with exactly one row
CredentialEnvironmentStateLast observed use
Finance accelerator · DEV
issued 26 Jul
 DEV active never observed
Showing all 1 credential

One credential per solution — re-issuing replaces the previous token rather than adding one. Last observed use is written best-effort and under-reports; it is not on its own sufficient to conclude a credential is dormant.

The strip does the work. A single row above a provenance strip reading showing all 1 credential is unambiguously complete. The same table with blank space below it is ambiguous, and ambiguity in a monitoring tool gets resolved as "broken".

Never pad with skeleton rows. Skeletons mean "loading". Using them to fill a sparse table tells an operator more data is coming when it is not — a small lie, on the screen whose entire premise is not telling them.

Never pad with sample rows. Same reasoning, larger consequence.

Tables keep their header and provenance strip at all densities, so a one-row table and a hundred-row table are recognisably the same component.

D4

The connection-health vocabulary

The probe returns exactly six values. dc2 shows a seventh that is not a probe result at all.

Specimen · the full scale
OK UNAUTHORIZED NOT_FOUND TIMEOUT ERROR NO_PROBE_PATH never tested
ValueRolls up asWhy
OKHealthyA real 200 was observed.
UNAUTHORIZEDNeeds attentionCredentials or the communication arrangement. Actionable by the customer.
NOT_FOUNDNeeds attentionThe service is not in this tenant.
TIMEOUTNeeds attentionReached, did not answer in time.
ERRORNeeds attentionThe tenant returned a failure.
NO_PROBE_PATHUnknownWe have no path to probe and refused to guess one. Our gap, not theirs.
nullOutside the scaleNo probe has run. The absence of a result, not a result.

NO_PROBE_PATH counts as unknown, never as needs-attention. It means we could not construct a probe path — the customer has nothing to fix. Rolling it into needs-attention would nag an operator about a gap on our side, and a screen that nags about things you cannot action is a screen people stop opening. The copy says no probe path configured, never failed.

NEVER_TESTED keeps its label and leaves the scale. It is the schema's name for a null column, not something the probe returns. It takes the dashed absence pill from D2 — same device, same meaning: a deliberate gap rather than an outcome.

Specimen · a failing connection that last worked three weeks ago
ConnectionHealthLast succeeded
Acme S/4
s4hana · prod
 TIMEOUT 6 Jul · 3 weeks ago

The column is "last succeeded", not "last validated". The timestamp moves only on a real 200, so a failing status beside an old date is the truth — it last worked three weeks ago and it is failing now. Naming the column for what the field means stops the pairing looking like a bug, and stops someone "fixing" it later by touching the timestamp on failure. That would destroy the only record of when the connection last actually worked.

Open questions

Not resolved here, because each is a product decision rather than a visual one.

  • Does a mismatch deserve to interrupt? It is rated critical by the incident rules and rendered as a chip here. Whether it also warrants a persistent page-level banner until acknowledged is a decision about how loud the product is willing to be — and there is no acknowledgement state in the data to hang it on.
  • What is the backlog denominator when there are no connections at all? The bar shows 9 of 12. At zero connections the honest reading is "nothing to declare yet", which is not the same as a cleared backlog, and the two must not render alike.
  • Should the latency basis line be suppressed when the sample is complete? It is kept always-on here on the argument that an inconsistent caveat is one people stop looking for. That trades a little noise for reliability, and it is a product call.
