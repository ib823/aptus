Dark theme contrast audit
Measured, not assumed

The focus ring still works
--focus-ring-navy is #8FB4E8 in dark — 8.67:1 on cream, 7.95:1 on paper. Navy stays the ring in both themes; red stays with irreversible actions.
Status colour is never alone
Every chip carries a glyph — ✓ i … ✕ – — so the six statuses survive both themes and colour blindness without relying on hue.
Shadows, not borders, carry depth
On a near-black ground a warm 4% shadow is invisible, so dark uses rgba(0,0,0,0.3–0.5) and leans on --surface-paper lifting off --surface-cream instead.
Status chips in dark · measured
– UnknownDraft
Unknown vs Draft — the pair that actually collides. Grounds 1.08:1 apart, both foregrounds warm grey. Fix: give Unknown a 1 px --border-strong outline and keep Draft flat; the – glyph then does the rest.
✕ SAP refused… No key
Refused vs No key — adequate, closer than in light. Grounds 1.05:1 apart; salmon against amber carries it. The ✕ and … glyphs work harder in dark than in light.
i No dataon navy-soft
No data on a navy-soft panel — 1.07:1. The chip nearly vanishes. Fix: never place a --status-sent chip on --brand-navy-soft in dark; panels stay --surface-paper, as above.
Across all fifteen pairs of the six status grounds, separation runs 1.01:1 to 1.10:1. In dark the ground carries almost no signal — every one is a near-black tint of the page — so the foreground and the glyph are the entire distinction. Light theme can lean on the ground; dark cannot, which makes the glyphs load-bearing rather than decorative.

One structural note. Three of these four were found only because the dark screens are a mechanical token map of the light ones — any colour the light theme hard-codes rather than tokenises shows up immediately as an unreadable pair. That is an argument for the light screens referencing tokens too, not for patching dark by hand.
