REQ-FIN-001
Standard SAP
Your ask
Generate monthly trial balance with comparative prior-period analysis.
What SAP does
Standard SAP produces this report exactly as described, with built-in comparison-period selection.
What it means for you
Nothing for your team to build. Available from day one of go-live.
REQ-FIN-014
Configurable
Your ask
Apply withholding tax to vendor payments per Inland Revenue Board (LHDN) Malaysia rules.
What SAP does
SAP supports country-specific withholding tax. Our team configures the LHDN rate matrix and certificate output.
What it means for you
Our team configures it once. Future rate changes are admin-level — no developer needed.
REQ-FIN-027
Trusted add-on
Your ask
Real-time FX exposure dashboard across MYR, USD, SGD, EUR positions with automated hedging recommendations.
What SAP does
Standard SAP tracks FX positions but does not recommend hedges. We deploy Kyriba, a treasury platform certified for SAP.
What it means for you
We deploy a certified third-party product (Kyriba). Licence cost ~MYR 320,000 / year. Lower risk than custom code.
REQ-FIN-041
Adapt to SAP
Your ask
Manual journal-entry approval flow with 4-tier authorisation matrix mirroring legacy paper process.
What SAP does
SAP standard supports a configurable approval workflow with up to 3 tiers. The 4th tier mirrors a paper-based control no longer required by audit.
What it means for you
We recommend adjusting the approval matrix to 3 tiers. SAP's standard approach is more efficient and avoids ongoing customisation cost.
