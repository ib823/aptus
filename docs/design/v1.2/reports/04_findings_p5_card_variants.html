Finding card — variants by outcome

All 8 outcome variants. Same card structure, different status dot, label, and "What it means for you" sentence (per §2.5 voice ladder).

REQ-FIN-001
Standard SAP
Your ask
Generate monthly trial balance with comparative prior-period analysis.
What SAP does
Standard SAP produces this report exactly as described, with built-in comparison-period selection.
What it means for you
Nothing to build. Available from day one of go-live.
REQ-FIN-014
Configurable
Your ask
Apply withholding tax to vendor payments per LHDN Malaysia rules.
What SAP does
SAP supports country-specific withholding tax. Our team configures the rate matrix and certificate output.
What it means for you
Our team configures it once. Future changes are admin-level — no developer needed.
REQ-FIN-041
Adapt to SAP
Your ask
Manual journal-entry approval flow with 4-tier authorisation mirroring legacy paper process.
What SAP does
SAP standard supports a configurable approval workflow with up to 3 tiers.
What it means for you
We recommend adjusting how this is handled today. Why: SAP's standard approach is more efficient and avoids ongoing customisation cost.
REQ-FIN-027
Trusted add-on
Your ask
Real-time FX exposure dashboard across MYR, USD, SGD, EUR with automated hedging recommendations.
What SAP does
Standard SAP tracks FX positions but does not recommend hedges.
What it means for you
We deploy a certified third-party product (Kyriba). Licence cost ~MYR 320,000 / year. Lower risk than custom code.
REQ-SD-082
Power-user extension
Your ask
Custom field on the sales order for "broker reference number" with copy-down to delivery and invoice.
What SAP does
SAP supports adding custom fields with copy-down behaviour through its no-code Key User Tools.
What it means for you
One of your power users builds it in SAP's no-code tool. About half a day of work, no developer required.
REQ-CSV-019
Cloud add-on (BTP)
Your ask
Customer-facing portal showing real-time order status, downloadable invoices, and a self-service returns request.
What SAP does
Core SAP exposes the data, but the public-facing portal is built outside core to keep it isolated.
What it means for you
We build a small companion app on SAP's cloud platform. About 8 days. Stays separate from core SAP — easier upgrades.
REQ-FIN-103
Custom build
Your ask
Bursa-specific clearing-house settlement engine integrating intra-day with the trading platform.
What SAP does
No standard SAP equivalent. Logic is unique to Bursa's competitive position as a market operator.
What it means for you
Unique to your business, so we build it in SAP. About 12 days. We've kept these to a minimum — only 1.4% of your requirements.
REQ-HR-058
Out of scope
Your ask
Talent-management succession planning with 360° feedback workflow.
What SAP does
Handled by SuccessFactors, a separate SAP product not selected for this phase.
What it means for you
Not addressed in this phase. Reason: SuccessFactors is targeted for Phase 2 (FY27). Can be revisited then.
